# Spectre case study: a red artifact is not a milestone

Spectre is a deterministic generative composition project with mechanical analysis gates and human
listening gates.

A fresh listening export was produced after a parent-genre restoration. The analyzer reported all
19 cohort members red and 108 failed cells against an explicit zero-cell budget. The export script
wrote MIDI and reports before returning failure, so the artifact directory existed even though the
canonical wrapper failed.

The initial interpretation tried to continue to listening because aggregates were nearly flat and
the prior cohort was also red. The governance correction was:

- classify the export as a failed diagnostic artifact;
- do not use it for an ear verdict;
- recompute old and new populations with the same current analyzer;
- report every retained, cleared, and new failure;
- repair failures by their distinct producer or measurement owners;
- require an explicit human exception rather than letting the planning agent waive the gate.

The lesson is that artifact existence, aggregate stability, and a plausible narrative do not
override a declared acceptance gate.
